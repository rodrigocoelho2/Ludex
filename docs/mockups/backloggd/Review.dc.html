Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 2025

Finished on PC 2 Oct → 9 Nov · 61 h

I did not expect a turn-based game to have me parrying like it was Sekiro. Every enemy turn is a tiny rhythm game, and by the second act I was dodging on instinct, eyes half on the animation and half on the music.

The music alone earns four stars. The last act earns the fifth: it takes the story somewhere I did not see coming and then trusts you to sit with it. I played it in long sessions with headphones on, and Lumière still sits in my head a week later.

If you bounced off JRPGs years ago because of slow menus and random battles, this is the one that brings you back. Short enough to finish, deep enough that I am already thinking about a second run.
